Gaadi Tracker vs a notebook and WhatsApp

Most Indian fleet owners run the hisab on a notebook, WhatsApp messages and phone calls. That works for one vehicle you drive yourself. Once other people drive your vehicles, the gaps show up in km, fuel and cash settlement. Here is an honest side-by-side.

Last updated 9 November 2026

When the notebook is enough

If you own one vehicle and drive it yourself, a notebook is fine. You know the km, you filled the fuel, and there’s nobody to settle with.

When it stops working

The day someone else drives. Now the km, the fuel and the cash come to you second-hand, and the notebook only holds what you were told. That is where Gaadi Tracker starts.
