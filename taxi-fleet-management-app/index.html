How Gaadi Tracker works for a cab fleet

Earnings by platform
You name the platforms: Uber, Ola, Rapido, local trips. The driver picks one and types the amount. Reports split revenue by platform.
Odometer in and out
Every shift starts and ends with a km reading, checked against the car’s last one. Km can’t go backwards.
CNG in kg, petrol in litres
The driver sees only the fuel types that car uses. CNG is entered in kg, the way the pump bills it.
Commission or rent, your call
Percentage, slab, daily rent or monthly salary, set per driver. The driver cost is worked out on every shift.
Approvals
Edited km, a past trip or a service record waits for you. Approve a shift and it’s locked.
Reports you can forward
Revenue, CNG, expenses, driver cost and profit by car, driver, platform or date range. Export as CSV or PDF.

Questions

Can I track Uber and Ola earnings separately for each car?

+

Yes. You add the platforms your drivers use, such as Uber, Ola, Rapido and local trips. The driver picks the platform each time they add earnings. Reports then show revenue by platform for each car, each driver or the whole fleet, for any date range, so you can see which platform pays best for each car.

What commission do cab owners usually give drivers?

+

It varies by city and by who pays for fuel. Many owners pay a percentage of the day’s revenue, others take a fixed daily rent and let the driver keep the rest, and some pay a monthly salary. Gaadi Tracker supports all three plus a slab model, and the calculator on our driver pay page shows what each one costs you on the same day.

Does the driver need internet to log a trip?

+

No. The Driver app works offline. Shifts, earnings, CNG, FASTag and expenses save on the phone and sync when the network returns. So the driver keeps working through basement parking, airport queues and dead zones.
