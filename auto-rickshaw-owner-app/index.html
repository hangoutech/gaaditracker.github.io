Why auto hisab goes wrong

  1. 01Rent is collected in cash, sometimes in parts, and the notebook doesn’t say which day was paid.
  2. 02A ₹500 advance for a tyre gets forgotten by one side.
  3. 03Km aren’t written down, so you can’t tell when the service is due.
  4. 04One driver drives two shifts on the same auto and the numbers get mixed.

How Gaadi Tracker works for autos

Daily rent
Set the rent per driver. The driver keeps whatever the auto earns above it, and you see the rent against every shift.
Advances and payments
Record an advance in your app; the driver records what they paid you in theirs. The balance shows “Balance to pay owner” or “Owner owes you”.
Km on every shift
Odometer in and out, checked against the last reading. Service reminders by km or date, whichever comes first.
LPG or CNG
The driver sees only the fuel your auto uses. CNG is entered in kg.
Rapido and local trips
Add the platforms your drivers use, including plain local trips.
Works without signal
Entries save on the phone and sync when the network returns.

Questions

How do I track daily rent from my auto drivers?

+

Set a daily rent rule for each driver in the Owner app. Every shift the driver records then shows the rent you are due and what the driver keeps. Cash the driver hands you is recorded as a payment, and any advance you give is recorded too, so the running balance tells both of you exactly where things stand.

Is it worth using an app for just two or three autos?

+

If you collect rent in cash and give the odd advance, yes. Small fleets lose money to missed days, forgotten advances and late services, not to big frauds. Gaadi Tracker keeps those small things on record, where both of you can see them.

Can I pay an auto driver a commission instead of charging rent?

+

Yes. Gaadi Tracker supports four pay models per driver: percentage of revenue, slab, fixed daily rent and monthly salary. You can switch a driver from rent to commission whenever you agree on it. Old shifts keep the old rule, so past hisab doesn’t change.
