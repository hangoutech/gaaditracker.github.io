How do the four pay models work?

Percentage of revenue

The driver gets a fixed share of what the vehicle earned that day. At 30%, Ravi’s ₹3,450 Uber day pays him ₹1,035. This is the most common arrangement for cabs on Uber and Ola because both sides share good days and slow ones.

Slab

You set a daily revenue slab and two rates. Below the slab, the lower rate applies to the whole day. At or above it, the higher rate applies to the whole day. With a ₹3,000 slab at 25% and 32%:

  • ₹2,800 day: ₹2,800 × 25% = ₹700
  • ₹3,450 day: ₹3,450 × 32% = ₹1,104

It gives a driver a clear reason to put in the extra trips that cross the slab.

Daily rent

The driver pays you a fixed amount each day and keeps whatever the vehicle earns above it. With ₹1,800 rent on a ₹3,450 day, you get ₹1,800 and the driver keeps ₹1,650. Common for autos and for cabs where the driver handles bookings.

Monthly salary

A fixed salary, spread over 26 working days. ₹18,200 a month is ₹700 a shift, whatever the revenue. Common for lorries, trucks, vans and buses, and for owners who want the earnings risk on their side.

What Gaadi Tracker does with the rule

Set one rule per driver in the Owner app. Each shift the driver records is costed with the rule in force when that shift started. If you change a rule, it applies from the next shift, and earlier shifts keep the old one, so past hisab doesn’t move. You can also back-date a rule to a chosen date.

Questions

How do I calculate a driver’s commission on Uber or Ola?

+

Take the day’s revenue the car earned, the amount the platform pays out for that driver’s trips, and multiply it by the agreed percentage. At 30% on ₹3,450, the driver’s commission is ₹1,035 and ₹2,415 stays with the owner before fuel and other expenses. Agree in advance whether fuel comes out before or after the split.

Is daily rent or commission better for the owner?

+

Rent gives the owner a fixed, predictable amount and puts the risk of a slow day on the driver. Commission shares both good and bad days. On a ₹3,450 day, ₹1,800 rent leaves the owner ₹1,800, while 30% commission leaves the owner ₹2,415. On a ₹2,200 day the same rent still pays ₹1,800, but 30% commission leaves ₹1,540.

Why is salary divided by 26 days?

+

A month has about 30 days, and drivers usually get around four days off, leaving roughly 26 working days. Gaadi Tracker divides the monthly salary by 26 to get a cost per shift. A ₹18,200 salary becomes ₹700 per shift, so a salaried driver shows up in the same per-day reports as a commission or rent driver.
