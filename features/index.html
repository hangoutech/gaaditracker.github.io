Features

Two apps. One for you, one for each driver.

Gaadi Tracker is two Android apps that share one account. Drivers record shifts, earnings, fuel and expenses in the Driver app. You see it all in the Owner app, set pay rules, approve changes and get reminders.

Gaadi Tracker Driver

What the driver does

One shift, start to finish. Follow the road.

  1. Before the shift
  2. 01

    Reminders

    A note before starting a shift when road tax or a service is due. It never blocks the shift.

  3. Start
  4. 02

    Start the shift

    Type the odometer reading. A start km below the vehicle’s last reading is blocked.

  5. On the road
  6. 03

    Earnings by platform

    Pick Uber, Ola, Rapido, local trips or any platform the owner has named, and type the amount. Lorries and trucks enter tons × rate per ton.

  7. 04

    Fuel

    Petrol, diesel, CNG in kg or LPG. Only the fuel types the vehicle uses are shown.

  8. 05

    FASTag and expenses

    Parking, service, repair, tyre, cleaning, app recharge or other, each in its own category.

  9. End of day
  10. 06

    End the shift

    Odometer reading out. An end km that isn’t higher than the start is blocked.

  11. 07

    Own balance

    “Balance to pay owner” or “Owner owes you”, with every advance and payment listed.

  12. Anytime
  13. 08

    Add a past trip

    Forgot to start a shift? Add it later, up to one year back. It goes to the owner for approval.

  14. 09

    Works offline

    Everything saves on the phone and syncs when the network returns.

  15. 10

    Your language

    English, Tamil, Hindi, Tanglish or Hinglish. Each driver picks the one they read best.

  16. Owner approves. Shift locked.

Gaadi Tracker Owner

What you see and control

Set it up once. Then just watch the numbers.

  1. Set up once
  2. 01

    Vehicles and drivers

    Add vehicles and drivers, create each driver’s login and assign a vehicle to each driver.

  3. 02

    Four pay models

    Percentage of revenue, slab, fixed daily rent or monthly salary over 26 working days. Changing a rule keeps old shifts on the old rule.

  4. Every day
  5. 03

    Dashboard

    Live shifts, today’s revenue and expenses, and alerts, across every vehicle.

  6. 04

    Push notifications

    When a shift starts or ends, and when a driver edits a trip.

  7. When it needs you
  8. 05

    Approvals

    Edited shift times or km, added past trips and recorded services wait for you. Reject puts the original back. Approve a shift to lock it.

  9. Stay ahead
  10. 06

    Service reminders

    By km or by date, whichever comes first.

  11. 07

    Road tax reminders

    Quarterly, for lorries, trucks, vans and buses, with “Mark paid”.

  12. Review
  13. 08

    Reports

    Revenue, fuel, expenses, driver cost and profit by vehicle, driver, platform or date range. Export as CSV or PDF.

  14. 09

    Fuel average

    Km per litre, or per kg for CNG, for lorries, trucks, vans and buses.

  15. 10

    Vehicle status

    Active, Inactive (off the road, keeps its driver) or Disabled (sold, kept in history).

  16. Every vehicle. Every rupee. One app.
